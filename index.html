Research

Research interests

My work spans trustworthy AI, geometric and topological learning, structured reasoning and causal machine learning.

AI reliability, safety, interpretability, uncertainty, autonomous agents and multi-agent systems.

Geometric & topological learning

Structured & knowledge-grounded AI

Causal & applied machine learning

About me

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Durham University, following an MSc in Business Analytics at Warwick and a BSc in Finance, Accounting and Management at Nottingham.

Before joining Kent, I conducted research at Cambridge on machine learning for neuroscience and at Oxford on large language models, knowledge graphs and reasoning. I have also worked in asset management and served as CTO at an AI-in-finance startup.

At Kent I teach and supervise across machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing and software development.
