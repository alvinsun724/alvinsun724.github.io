01 / Research

Questions I work on

Across my work, one question recurs: what makes the internal structure of a learned system useful for generalisation, reasoning and reliability?

01

Trustworthy, reliable and agentic AI

How can we recognise when a model or autonomous agent is likely to fail? I study reliability, uncertainty, monitoring and long-horizon behaviour, including the challenges that arise when agents use tools and interact with one another.

02

Geometric and topological representation learning

What can the shape and structure of representations tell us about learning? My work investigates topology, geometry, graph and hypergraph learning, sheaf-based approaches and the dynamics of neural representations.

03

Structured, neuro-symbolic and knowledge-grounded AI

How can learning systems combine statistical representations with structured knowledge and compositional reasoning? Current directions include knowledge graphs, graph logic, structured memory, large language models and world models.

04

Causal learning and dependable decisions

How do models behave under distribution shift and changing conditions? I develop and apply causal and reliable learning methods in financial systems, healthcare and education, where errors can have tangible consequences.

04 / Opportunities

For prospective researchers

I welcome conversations with PhD applicants, research students, postdoctoral researchers and colleagues who enjoy tackling difficult questions in machine learning.

You do not need to arrive with a fully formed project. It helps to have a specific question you care about, familiarity with recent literature and a willingness to test ideas rigorously. Three directions are especially interesting to me at present:

RESEARCH DIRECTION / 01

When can an AI agent be trusted?

Investigate failure detection, evidence, monitoring and adaptation in language-model agents and multi-agent systems. Suitable for researchers interested in LLMs, evaluation, agent development and statistical reasoning.

RESEARCH DIRECTION / 02

What does a model's geometry reveal?

Study representation dynamics, memorisation, topology, graph structure or generalisation. Suitable for researchers interested in PyTorch, geometric deep learning, graph theory and careful experimentation.

RESEARCH DIRECTION / 03

How can AI reason with structure?

Explore neuro-symbolic learning, knowledge-grounded models, world models and structured memory. Potential applications include finance, healthcare and scientific AI.

Interested in working together?

Please email a short CV, links to relevant research or code, and a paragraph outlining one question you would like to investigate. For external doctoral co-supervision, include your host institution and supervisor where applicable.

Get in touch ↗

I am open to discussing joint projects, external co-supervision and potential research visits through existing and new academic or industry collaborations. These are possibilities to explore, rather than advertised funded places or guaranteed internships; individual arrangements depend on partner agreement, capacity and funding.

05 / About

Background & service

Academic background

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Durham University, and hold degrees in business analytics and finance-related disciplines. Before joining Kent, I conducted research at Cambridge on machine learning for neuroscience and at Oxford on language models, knowledge graphs and reasoning. My earlier experience in asset management also informs my work on AI in finance.

Alongside research and supervision, I teach machine learning, natural language processing, deep learning and software development at Kent.
