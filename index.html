01 — Research

Areas of interest.

I study machine learning through complementary perspectives on representation, structure and reliability. These broad themes connect my published work and ongoing academic interests.

01

Trustworthy & agentic AI

AI reliability, safety, evaluation and autonomous systems, including language models and multi-agent AI.

02

Geometric & topological learning

Representation learning through geometry, topology, graphs, hypergraphs, sheaves and related mathematical structures.

03

Structured & neuro-symbolic AI

Knowledge-grounded learning, structured reasoning, knowledge graphs and the interface between symbolic and neural methods.

04

Causal & reliable machine learning

Causal methods, robustness and AI-assisted decision-making, with applications in finance, healthcare and education.

Beyond research.

Background, academic community involvement and selected public-facing work.

About me

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Durham University, following degrees in Business Analytics at Warwick and Finance, Accounting and Management at Nottingham. I previously worked on machine learning for neuroscience at Cambridge and on language models and knowledge graphs at Oxford.

Before academia I worked in asset management. At Kent I teach machine learning, natural language processing, deep learning and software development, alongside research and supervision.
